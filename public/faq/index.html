FAQ

Common questions about Vedic astrology

Astrology can offer reflective insight into personality, cycles, and decision-making. This FAQ helps visitors understand the purpose, scope, and value of a Vedic chart reading.

Quick answers

  • What is a birth chart? A map of planetary positions at the time of birth.
  • What is a moon sign? A sign-based reflection of emotional tendencies and inner patterns.
  • How are charts calculated? Planetary positions are interpreted with Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses.
  • Is astrology scientific? It is generally treated as symbolic and spiritual guidance, not a scientific prediction system.
  • How is my information handled? The report is processed in your browser; place search sends only the place name to Open-Meteo.

Who is Vedic astrology for?

It is for anyone curious about deeper self-understanding, life themes, relationship patterns, and timing cycles. Many users approach it as a reflective and spiritual framework rather than a fixed truth.

What should I expect from a reading?

A reading offers symbolic themes for reflection. It is for guidance and entertainment only, not a prediction or professional advice.
