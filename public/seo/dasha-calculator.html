Dasha calculator

In Vedic astrology, a Dasha period refers to a planetary cycle that shapes the timing of life events. These phases are used to help explain which areas of life may come into focus over a period of time.

What Dashas can indicate

  • Major life chapters and transitions.
  • Career or relationship timing.
  • Periods of growth, challenge, and reset.
  • When action or patience may be especially relevant.

Why timing matters

A birth chart describes the overall themes in life, while Dashas help show the timing of those themes. They support greater clarity on when certain opportunities, lessons, or changes may become more visible.

Common learning goals

  • Understanding life chapters.
  • Finding periods of growth and challenge.
  • Preparing with more awareness for transitions.
  • Matching timing with decisions and planning.
