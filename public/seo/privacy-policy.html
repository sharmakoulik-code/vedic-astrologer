Privacy

Privacy policy

Birth details are processed in your browser to display the chart and reading. If you search for a birth place, only the place name you enter is sent to Open-Meteo for location lookup.

Core principle

  • The report is calculated and displayed in your browser.
  • Place search sends only the entered place name to Open-Meteo.
  • Open-Meteo returns location results to the browser for the chart.

Information collected

The name, birth date, time, and location details you enter are used in your browser to calculate and display the reading.

How it is used

The only external lookup is the optional birth-place search. It sends the place name you enter to Open-Meteo; the search result, including coordinates and timezone when available, is returned to your browser.
