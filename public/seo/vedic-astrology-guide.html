Vedic astrology guide

Vedic astrology, also known as Jyotish, is an ancient system of understanding life through the positions of planets, the zodiac, and the birth chart. It helps people explore patterns in personality, timing, purpose, relationships, and decision-making.

What is Vedic astrology?

This site's chart calculations use planetary positions, Lahiri ayanamsa, and whole-sign houses. Interpretations are for guidance and entertainment, not certain predictions.

It is commonly used to understand personality, emotional needs, strengths, relationship patterns, financial tendencies, and the timing of major life events.

Key elements in a reading

  • Birth date and time.
  • Birth place and local sky context.
  • Planetary positions and houses.
  • Personalized interpretation of life themes.

How Vedic astrology is often used

Many people use astrology for self-awareness, planning, relationship insights, professional direction, and timing awareness. It is most useful when treated as a reflective tool that complements practical judgment rather than replacing it.
