Birth chart basics

Birth chart calculator

A birth chart, or natal chart, represents planetary positions for a birth date and time. This site's calculations use Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses; interpretations are for guidance and entertainment.

Generate My Birth Chart Read the guide

Birth details matter

  • Birth date is used to calculate planetary positions.
  • Birth time and timezone are needed to calculate Lagna.
  • Birth place and coordinates are needed for Lagna and house placements.
  • Without a timezone, Lagna and house placements are omitted.

How the chart is formed

Planetary positions are calculated from the birth date and time using Lahiri ayanamsa. Whole-sign houses are used when the birth time, timezone, and location are available. The interpretation is symbolic, for guidance and entertainment only.

Why people use it

A birth chart is often used for self-understanding and planning. It can help identify natural strengths, relationship dynamics, career tendencies, and periods of growth or challenge.
