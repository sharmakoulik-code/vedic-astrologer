Methodology

Astrology methodology

Chart calculations use planetary positions interpreted with Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. Readings are symbolic and intended for guidance and entertainment.

Core principles

  • Planetary positions are calculated from the entered birth date and time.
  • Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses are used.
  • Lagna and houses require a birth time, timezone, and location.
  • Interpretations are for guidance and entertainment, not certain predictions.

What is reflected in the report

The reading commonly includes personality themes, relationship patterns, career tendencies, financial habits, timing cycles, and general life direction. These are derived from the chart and interpreted with traditional astrological frameworks.

Important disclaimer

For guidance and entertainment only. Not medical, legal, financial or professional advice.
