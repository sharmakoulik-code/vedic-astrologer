Methodology

Astrology methodology

This platform is designed around traditional Vedic astrology principles. Birth details are used to chart planetary placements, identify key house themes, and provide contextual interpretation for personality, timing, and life direction.

Core principles

  • Birth date, time, and place are used to determine the chart.
  • Planetary positions are interpreted within the framework of Vedic astrology.
  • House themes and life areas guide the reading.
  • Interpretations are symbolic, reflective, and contextual.

What is reflected in the report

The reading commonly includes personality themes, relationship patterns, career tendencies, financial habits, timing cycles, and general life direction. These are derived from the chart and interpreted with traditional astrological frameworks.

Important disclaimer

Astrology interpretations are not scientifically established predictions. They are best treated as a reflective spiritual or symbolic framework for self-awareness, insight, and decision support, not as certainty. This tool should not replace medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.
