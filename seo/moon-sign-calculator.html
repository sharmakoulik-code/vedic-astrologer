Moon sign calculator

The moon sign reveals your emotional and instinctive nature. In Vedic astrology, it often highlights how you process feelings, how you seek comfort, and what brings you inner stability.

Moon sign insight

  • Reflects emotional reactions and inner security.
  • Shows how you comfort yourself and others.
  • Helps explain instinctive behavior and personal needs.
  • Supports understanding of relationship dynamics.

Why the moon matters

While the Sun sign often reflects outward expression, the Moon sign often reflects private inner life. It can explain emotional patterns, reaction styles, and the comfort-seeking instinct behind your choices.

What the moon sign can reveal

  • How you handle stress and uncertainty.
  • What makes you feel emotionally safe.
  • How you respond in relationships and family settings.
  • What kind of inner growth is most meaningful.
