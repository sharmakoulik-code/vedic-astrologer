Birth chart basics

Birth chart calculator

A birth chart, or natal chart, maps the sky at the exact moment you were born. It shows the placement of the planets across the twelve houses and gives insight into your personality, temperament, and life themes.

Birth details matter

  • Exact birth date influences the planetary positions.
  • Birth time affects the ascendant and house divisions.
  • Birth place shapes the local sky and chart geometry.
  • Personal chart readings become more precise with accurate inputs.

How the chart is formed

Your birth chart is built using your birth date, exact time, and location. These details help determine your ascendant, house placements, and planetary positions. From there, the reading can reveal themes related to purpose, relationships, money, health, and timing.

Why people use it

A birth chart is often used for self-understanding and planning. It can help identify natural strengths, relationship dynamics, career tendencies, and periods of growth or challenge.
